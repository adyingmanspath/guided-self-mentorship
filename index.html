01 Is this you?

You know the pattern.
You still can’t seem to change it.

You’re self-aware. You can usually explain why you’re overthinking, putting things off, or doubting yourself. You may even notice the pattern while it’s happening and feel so frustrated that you still can’t seem to change it.

You’ve tried therapy. Waking up earlier. Affirmations. The book. The journal. Meditation. The app. You make a plan to finally fix everything, and you mean it.

Then you either fall short, or do everything right just to discover that you’re putting in more effort without feeling any better about your life.

You end up wondering if you’re lazy, not smart enough, or if there’s just something fundamentally wrong with you.

I’ve been there. It’s painful. And it isn’t proof that you’re defective. What keeps most people stuck isn’t a lack of awareness. It’s that we haven’t been taught how to understand what keeps the pattern going—or what to do about it. These patterns have reasons. We find them, then practice something different.

02 The method

You learn to become your own mentor.
I guide you while you learn.

Guided Self-Mentorship is a structured and comprehensive set of tools to understand your challenges, know what truly matters to you, and find actionable steps to apply what you’ve learned in your life.

I guide you through it first, using it on one of your greatest challenges so you can begin finding relief in the first session. Then you practice it where your life actually happens—during the difficult conversation, the unproductive Tuesday, and the moment your brain starts holding a three-hour committee meeting.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Get beneath the obvious problem and understand what is keeping it going.

  2. 02

    Choose

    Get clear on what matters to you—not what shame, panic, or everyone else says should matter.

  3. 03

    Act

    Turn what you understand into a practical next step, then learn from what actually happens.

Your first two hours

We do more than talk about the problem.

Before we meet, you complete an individualized assessment so I understand what’s making life hard, what’s already working, and what you need from our time.

In the session, we get to the root, learn Guided Self-Mentorship, and use it on your greatest challenge. You leave with relief, a clearer understanding of yourself, and practical tools to navigate your life.

Inside the Path to Living Well

You learn Guided Self-Mentorship in the first session and use it throughout. It is the foundation, not the entire house. Other sessions teach additional tools chosen around your assessment, your life, and what will actually help.

06 About your guide

My greatest qualification is that
I’ve been where you are.

This is the part where I’m supposed to list my credentials. They’re below. The more useful truth is that I know how it feels to work hard and still believe you are the problem.

For the first twenty-five years of my life, I was anxious and depressed. I didn’t want to live. At my lowest, I hid in video games for sixteen hours a day. Games were the one place I knew what to do and got to feel successful.

Getting kicked out of university was humiliating and, inconveniently, the beginning of my life. I stopped asking what was wrong with me and started asking why my struggles made sense. Understanding changed what I could do. Action changed how I lived.

Life is a joy now. Slow mornings with coffee and a cat. Long hikes. Learning things for no reason. Food that’s irresponsibly spicy.

I trained as a therapist and spent the next decade helping other people move from suffering to lives they actually enjoy. Watching it happen has left me with a borderline ridiculous, possibly delusional belief that anyone can recover. Ten years in, nothing has talked me out of it.

My own life taught me half of this. The people I’ve sat with taught me the rest. That’s what qualifies me most, not the letters below.

Licensed therapist and LCSW, 10+ years of experience. Master’s in Social Work, University of Wisconsin–Madison.

07 Common questions

Before you decide.

What exactly is Guided Self-Mentorship?+

It is a structured set of tools for understanding why you are stuck, clarifying what matters to you, and finding an action that makes sense in your actual life. I guide you through the process first. You then practice it until you can mentor yourself through future challenges.

What is the difference between Guided Self-Mentorship and the Path?+

Guided Self-Mentorship is a focused four-week service for one specific part of life. The Path to Living Well is an eight-session, sixteen-week program for people who want deeper change across their lives. The Path starts by teaching Guided Self-Mentorship, uses it throughout, and adds other tools chosen around your individual needs.

I’m looking for a therapist in Chiang Mai. Is that you?+

I’m a licensed therapist with over ten years of experience, based in Chiang Mai. I offer support using the skills I learned as a therapist, but I now operate as a guide instead. I don’t diagnose, treat illness, or take insurance. We focus on understanding your challenges in order to find wellness.

What kinds of challenges can this help with?+

People often come with self-doubt, procrastination, perfectionism, overthinking, anxiety, burnout, difficult emotions, relationship patterns, habits, distraction, or the feeling that their life should be working better than it is. The tools are adaptable because we begin with your specific life—not a prewritten diagnosis.

Will I really feel relief in the first session?+

The first session is designed for it, and most measured participants reported substantial immediate improvement. That isn’t a promise that every person will feel the same way or that everything will be solved in two hours. It means we create enough time to understand something important, use the tools, and find out whether they are actually helping.

How much work happens between sessions?+

Plan for roughly 30–45 minutes of practice most days at first. Some of that is the 7-Day Path exercise; some is doing something differently in ordinary life. The exercise gets shorter with practice. It is not a hidden cost, and it is not optional busywork. This is where knowing becomes something you can actually do.

Are you saying I’ll feel good permanently?+

No. Nobody feels good permanently, and trying to can become its own exhausting job. The goal is to understand what difficult feelings are telling you, know how to respond, and trust that you can find your way back to relief and joy.

What if it doesn’t help?+

If you attend your sessions, complete the between-session work and pre/post surveys, and you aren’t seeing change or are otherwise dissatisfied, I give you a full refund. The point is for this to help—not for you to become poorer with excellent attendance.

Is this available online and worldwide?+

Yes. I’m based in Chiang Mai, Thailand, and offer both services online worldwide. We find session times that work across time zones.

What happens after I apply?+

I read your application myself. Then we talk about what you’re looking for, whether this is a good fit, and which option makes sense. Applying doesn’t commit you to paying or starting.
